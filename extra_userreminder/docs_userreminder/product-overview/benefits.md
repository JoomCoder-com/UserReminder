# Benefits

## More finished sign-ups

A reminder with the activation link again brings back people who missed or lost the first email. You decide how many reminders they get and how far apart.

## Members come back

Users who never logged in, or stopped visiting, get a short email with a link to your site. You choose the wording in Joomla's mail templates, in every site language.

## A user list you can trust

Accounts that never react can be deleted after the last reminder, or moved out of a group. Your user counts, newsletters and exports then reflect real people.

## Nothing leaves your site

Emails go through your own Joomla mail settings. User data stays in your database. There is no outside service, no per-user fee and no tracking.

## You stay in control

- Every reminder type is off until you switch it on
- Every list shows what the next run will do for each user, before it happens
- A dry-run mode shows exactly what would be sent, without sending
- Every reminder, opt-out and resubscribe is in the action log

## Respectful by default

Every email carries an unsubscribe link. The link opens a confirmation page, so a mail scanner that follows links does not unsubscribe anyone. Staff groups can be left out completely.
